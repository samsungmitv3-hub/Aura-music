SP MUSIC for iOS

Google Drive Folder Streaming

1. Connect Public Google Drive Folder Set your Google Drive music folder sharing to "Anyone with the link", paste the Folder URL/ID and your Google Drive API v3 Key in Settings, and tap Link & Play Folder.

2. Persistent Sequential Playback Your folder stays linked in localStorage across reloads—automatically playing sequentially through all audio files until you manually tap Unlink.

3. Install as Native iOS App Open in Safari on iPhone/iPad, tap Share, and choose "Add to Home Screen".
